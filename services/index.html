01

Energy performance modelling

BREEAM International NC V6 and V7 Ene 01 · V7 Ene 02 · LEED v4.1 BD+C EA Minimum and Optimize Energy Performance · LEED v5 BD+C EAp1, EAp2, EAc2, EAc3

When you need it
The credit route needs whole-building energy analysis, or a demand and sizing model has to become a detailed consumption model.
What we own
Baseline and design models with envelope, internal loads, HVAC, controls and the relevant unregulated uses. Comparisons of defined design options. The calculators and technical narrative for the agreed scope.
When to start
From design-informing analysis through technical design. As-built update when the scheme asks for it.
You receive
The models, the results, an assessment report per credit and the supporting narrative.
Stays outside
Local regulatory calculations, coordination of the full certification and ownership of the design, unless agreed in writing.
Proof
Two office renovations, Lisbon — LEED v4.1 Appendix G.

To quote, send: drawings, scheme and version, assessment type, design stage and a description of the HVAC systems.

02

Thermal comfort and climate resilience

BREEAM International NC V6 Hea 04 · V7 Hea 05 · LEED v4.1 BD+C EQ Thermal Comfort · LEED v5 BD+C EQc2

When you need it
The project targets a comfort or adaptability credit, or the team wants to know whether spaces will overheat now and under future weather.
What we own
Dynamic comfort and overheating analysis to PMV/PPD or the applicable standard. Current and future weather scenarios. Tests of defined passive or control adaptations. Model-based input to zoning and controls discussions.
When to start
Before the comfort strategy and the controls are fixed. Technical-design evidence and later updates can follow.
You receive
An assessment report with results by zone, the adaptations tested and, where scoped, the design-to-as-built update.
Stays outside
We analyse performance and options. The MEP designer keeps the system design and owns the control-system design.
Proof
East Gate Business Park Pro B2 — BREEAM Hea 04, 64 zones.

To quote, send: drawings, space uses, glazing and shading, ventilation strategy and the project location.

03

Early design decision support

LEED v4.1 Integrative Process · BREEAM early-stage requirements in Ene 02 and Ene 04 · early comfort decisions

When you need it
At concept or schematic design, while form, orientation, envelope and systems are still open.
What we own
Simple-box or reduced-complexity energy analysis. Comparison of defined options. Early targets and modelling assumptions. A check of which credits on your route need action now.
When to start
Concept or schematic design. Later, most of what this service protects is already decided.
You receive
A short comparison of the options tested and an evidence plan for the credits still open. This is the Credit Viability Read.
Stays outside
The design decisions stay with the design team. We quote this narrowly, around the decisions to be tested.
Proof
Method and selected parts of past projects. A dedicated case is being prepared.

To quote, send: the concept drawings, the decisions still open and the target rating.

04

Documentation, QA and submission support

Runs across every service above

When you need it
Always, in practice. It is what makes a model reviewable by your team and defensible in front of an assessor.
What we own
Assumptions and input registers. QA of the model and the results. Assessment narratives and evidence. Calculator and platform inputs where included. Answers to technical comments within the agreed scope.
When to start
With the modelling. Comment support is scoped explicitly in the quote.
You receive
Documents under the R2F name or yours, in your template if you work white-label.
Stays outside
Managing the certification and any promise of assessor acceptance.
Proof
Both cases.

Three ways to collaborate

The scope is the same. What changes is whose name is on the work and who talks to whom.

Direct

R2F is named on the reports and works with your project team as the modelling consultant.

Alongside your team

We take a defined part of the modelling while your engineers keep the rest of the scope.

White-label

Your brand, your templates, your client. We stay behind your process.

What we check before results leave

Structured QA on every model, with the assumptions written down. Python scripts handle extraction and repetitive checks. The engineering judgement is ours.

  • Geometry, floor areas, wall areas and volumes
  • Thermal zone adjacencies
  • Constructions and material assignments
  • Internal load densities and schedules
  • EnergyPlus errors and warnings
  • Unmet hours
  • Energy balance and load components
  • HVAC sizing and performance
  • Temperature and humidity profiles
