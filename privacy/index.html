Privacy

This site is run by R2F Energy Efficient Solutions LLC. Questions about your data go to roberto@r2f.solutions.

What we collect

Only what you send us: the answers in the Credit Viability Read form, or the content of an email you write to us. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Why

To reply to your request and, if you ask for it, to prepare a quote.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered by Formspree, which forwards them to our email. Emails are stored in our Google Workspace account. The site is hosted by Hostinger.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer and follow up on your request. Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
