R2F / Cases / Lisbon

Two office renovations, Lisbon

A sustainability partner needed specialist capacity to complete LEED v4.1 Appendix G energy modelling for two complex office renovations. R2F built and iterated the proposed and baseline models for both buildings and prepared the technical evidence for submission.

Scheme
LEED v4.1 BD+C Core and Shell
Credits
EA Minimum Energy Performance · Optimize Energy Performance
Buildings
Two office renovations · about 18,000 m² conditioned
Model
Proposed and baseline, whole building · OpenStudio, EnergyPlus
R2F role
Detailed energy modelling and documentation, within the partner's process
Delivery
White-label · July 2025

Fig. 1
The Appendix G workflow, run once per building.

What we did

  1. Modelled both buildings in detail

    Whole-building proposed models with several HVAC system types, envelope, lighting, service hot water, lifts and on-site generation.

  2. Built the Appendix G baselines

    Baseline models generated to the Appendix G rules for each building.

  3. Iterated with the partner

    Proposed and baseline models revised as the design information evolved.

  4. Prepared the evidence

    The technical documentation for the LEED energy credits, in the partner's process.

What this case shows

  • A complex LEED energy package, handled in detail.
  • Work done inside another consultant's process and brand.

What we don't publish
