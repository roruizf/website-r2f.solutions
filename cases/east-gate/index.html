East Gate Business Park Pro B2

An industrial and office development needed a thermal comfort assessment that would hold up under current and future climate. R2F built the dynamic model, tested the comfort criteria and documented the assessment for the project team.

Scheme
BREEAM International New Construction V6
Issue
Hea 04 · thermal comfort and adaptability to climate change
Building
Warehouse and office areas
Model
64 thermal zones · OpenStudio, EnergyPlus
R2F role
Responsible for the dynamic thermal model and the assessment report
Delivery
Under the R2F name

Fig. 1
The assessment sequence. One zone, one hot week, three runs.

What we did

  1. Built the dynamic model

    A 64-zone OpenStudio and EnergyPlus model covering the warehouse and the office areas.

  2. Ran current and future climate

    The same model, run with current weather and with future weather scenarios.

  3. Tested the comfort criteria

    Results checked against the Hea 04 criteria, zone by zone.

  4. Documented the assessment

    An assessment report written for the project team and the assessor.

What this case shows

  • R2F can own a defined BREEAM modelling package from model to report.

What we don't publish

  • The rating, the credits awarded or zone results.
  • Drawings, renders or report pages.
