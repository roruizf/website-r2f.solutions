When teams bring us in

Usually it is one of these five. If none fits, write anyway and describe the project.

  1. A BREEAM or LEED modelling package has to be delivered, and nobody on the team models every day.
  2. The team is at capacity for a phase or a single project.
  3. A demand or sizing model has to become a detailed consumption model.
  4. The design team wants model-based evidence before decisions get expensive to change.
  5. A submission, a revision or an assessor comment needs structured modelling support.

Some credits are won at concept.

Evidence is submitted at design stage and checked after construction. When the modelling starts decides which credits are still open.

  1. Concept

    Building form and early energy strategy. Options are still cheap to test.

  2. Schematic

    Fabric, low and zero carbon options, a first comfort check. Before the envelope is frozen.

  3. Technical design

    Final energy and comfort models. The design-stage evidence is written here.

  4. Post-construction

    As-built check. Often a confirmation, sometimes a model update.

Not sure what is still open on your project? Ask for a Credit Viability Read.

How a project runs

  1. Intake

    You send the drawings and the minimum data from our intake table. We confirm in writing when it is complete.

  2. Fixed fee

    A fee per building and per credit, with a confirmed start date.

  3. First result on a set date

    Diagnosis and recommendations for your priority credit, on the date written in the quote.

  4. Iterations

    Two rounds of design iteration included. Changing glazing, U-values or set-points is an iteration, not new scope.

  5. Evidence and comments

    Reports written for the assessor. We answer every question about our work, from you, the assessor, BRE or USGBC, until it is accepted. No extra fee.

  6. As-built

    Post-construction review, quoted separately.

Questions we get

Do you publish prices?

No. You get a range early, and a fixed fee per building and credit once the minimum data is in. Every quote lists what is included and what is not.

Can you work under our brand?

Yes. We use your templates and your name on the reports, and we stay out of your client relationship.

What do you need to start?

Drawings, the scheme and version, the assessment type, the design stage and a description of the systems. At intake we send a short table of the minimum data and who usually provides it.

How long until the first result?

It depends on the size and complexity of the building and the first credit. The date is written in the quote, and the clock starts when the minimum data is complete.

Do you guarantee the credits?

No one honestly can: the certifier awards them. We guarantee our own work. If the assessor or BRE finds an error in it, we fix it until it is accepted.

Which software do you use?

OpenStudio and EnergyPlus, with our own scripts for setup and QA.

Where do you work?

Remotely, with teams across Europe, in English, French or Spanish.
